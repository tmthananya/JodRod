Privacy Policy

Application: JodRod • Package / Identifier: com.tmthananya.jodrod
Effective Date: October 22, 2026 • Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Welcome to JodRod (จอดรถ) ("we," "our," or "us"). JodRod is a civic, non-profit community utility designed to help vehicle owners evacuate their cars to safe elevated ground during flood emergencies and to enable property owners and community members to offer safe parking spaces.

This Privacy Policy explains how JodRod collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our web platform and mobile application (available on Android via Google Play and iOS).

Our Civic Commitment: JodRod is built exclusively for emergency flood parking coordination. We do not display advertisements, we do not track your background GPS movements, and we will never sell or monetize your personal information.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Collected

We collect only the minimal data strictly necessary to facilitate safe parking discovery, reservations, and community coordination. All collected information is voluntarily provided by you through the application interface:

Category Data Fields Collected Collection Purpose
Account Credentials Email address and password Account registration, secure authentication, password resets, and session management via Supabase Auth.
Community Profile Display name and selected preset avatar identifier Identifying you to other community members when coordinating parking spaces or reservations.
Vehicle Information License plate number and vehicle model/description Allowing parking space owners to recognize your vehicle upon arrival and prevent unauthorized parking.
Parking Space Listings Parking space name/landmark, manual pin-drop coordinates (latitude and longitude), vehicle capacity, available spots, and availability status Displaying available emergency parking spots on the community map to neighbors during floods.
Reservation Requests Selected parking spot, associated vehicle, status (confirmed, cancelled, completed), and optional arrival notes Mutual confirmation of parking slots between drivers and space providers.
Technical Session Data Essential session cookie (PHPSESSID) and CSRF security token Maintaining secure login state and protecting web requests against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF).

2. Information We Do Not Collect

To maintain transparency and protect user privacy, we explicitly verify that JodRod does not collect or process:

  • No Background GPS Tracking: We do not track your device's physical location silently or in the background. Geographic coordinates for parking spaces are designated manually by the space owner when dropping a pin or selecting a spot on the interactive map.
  • No Payment or Financial Data: JodRod is a free civic mutual-aid system. We do not process payments, fees, credit cards, or banking information.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: We do not access your device's camera, microphone, or audio hardware.
  • No Personal Photo File Uploads: Avatars in JodRod are selected from pre-configured preset illustrations; we do not read your photo library or upload image files.
  • No Contacts or Device Phonebook: We do not inspect, read, or upload your address book or contacts.
  • No Advertising or Behavioral Trackers: We do not run third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, or user profiling trackers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for civic emergency relief operations, specifically to:

  • Allow vehicle owners in flood-prone areas to discover safe, elevated parking spaces on a real-time interactive map.
  • Allow property owners and volunteers to register, update capacity, and close their available parking spaces.
  • Facilitate mutual reservation requests and arrival verification between drivers and parking hosts.
  • Verify authenticated user accounts and safeguard account security.
  • Prevent duplicate reservations, abuse, and vandalism of community listings.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, marketing campaigns, behavioral profiling, or automated credit scoring.

4. Storage, Hosting, and Database Services

JodRod stores user accounts, profiles, vehicle records, parking listings, and reservation requests in a cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database managed by Supabase Inc.

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Our database implements strict Row Level Security policies. Users can only edit or delete their own listings, vehicles, profiles, and reservations.
  • Backend Credential Isolation: Database access credentials and API service tokens are securely stored on our backend server environment and are never exposed in client-side code or public repositories.
  • Web Application Hosting: The JodRod web service serves client requests, APIs, and the mobile WebView interface.

5. Information Sharing and Categories of Recipients

Because JodRod is a community-driven mutual aid network, certain information is shared among community members to fulfill the app's purpose:

  • Public Community Information: Parking space names, geographic pin coordinates (latitude/longitude), total capacity, and available slot counts are publicly visible on the map to all users searching for flood parking. Community display names and preset avatars are visible on public member lists.
  • Reservation Coordination: When you reserve a parking space, the owner of that parking space can view the reservation details, including the assigned vehicle license plate, vehicle model, and any arrival notes you submitted, to verify that your vehicle is permitted to enter.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: We share data with trusted infrastructure providers who assist in operating our platform:
    • Supabase Inc.: Cloud database hosting and user authentication provider.
    • OpenStreetMap Foundation: Map tile provider for rendering map layers in Leaflet. Standard HTTP requests (including your IP address and user-agent) are sent to OpenStreetMap tile servers when map tiles are downloaded.
  • No Sale or Commercial Transfer: We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize any user personal data with data brokers, advertisers, or commercial third parties.
  • Legal Requirements: We will only disclose your information if required to do so by applicable law, court order, or official governmental request.

6. Data Retention and Your Deletion Rights

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to maintain your community parking listings.

Your Rights and In-App Self-Service Deletion

JodRod provides built-in tools allowing you to manage and delete your personal information directly within the application:

  • Delete Vehicles: You can remove any registered vehicle at any time from the "My Vehicles" section.
  • Delete Parking Spaces: Parking space hosts can remove their listings at any time from the "My Parking Lots" section.
  • Delete Profile: You can permanently remove your member profile from the "My Profile" section.
  • Complete Account Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your entire account, authentication credentials, and all associated data, you may submit an account deletion request using the contact email provided below. Once processed, your account and all linked records (vehicles, reservations, and profile) are permanently purged from the database via cascade deletion.

7. Security Practices

We employ standard industry security measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • Cryptographic Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Supabase Auth handles passwords using industry-standard salted cryptographic hashing algorithms.
  • Database Access Control: Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) ensures that write and update operations are strictly restricted to the authentic owner of each record.
  • CSRF Protection: All state-changing web and API requests are protected using cryptographically random Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) tokens.
  • Cookie Security: Session cookies are configured with HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax security attributes, and Secure flags when transmitted over HTTPS.

Security Limitation Disclaimer: While we take standard reasonable technical measures to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot warrant or guarantee absolute security.

8. Device Permissions (Android & iOS)

The JodRod mobile application (Android package: com.tmthananya.jodrod; iOS bundle identifier: com.tmthananya.jodrod) requests only minimal system privileges:

  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required for connecting to the application server, fetching active parking space data, and loading OpenStreetMap map tiles.
  • Network State (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to detect network connectivity and assist with offline/reconnection status.

We do not request dangerous or sensitive runtime permissions such as camera, microphone, background location, contact access, or external storage access.

10. Children's Privacy

JodRod is designed for motor vehicle drivers, property owners, and community coordinators. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

11. International Data Transfers

Our infrastructure providers (such as Supabase) may store and process data in cloud servers located in multiple geographic regions. If you access JodRod from outside these regions, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in jurisdictions that may have different data protection laws than your home country.
